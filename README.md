## 🎉 What is this?

Tambola (also known as Housie or Bingo) is the ultimate family and party game.
This project gives you **everything you need to host a game night**, using nothing but two static HTML files:

| 🎙️ Host | 🎟️ Players |
|---|---|
| Opens the **Game Board** on a big screen or laptop | Open the **Ticket page** on their own phones |
| Calls numbers with one click | Get a randomly generated ticket |
| Tracks the winners | Tap numbers to cross them off |

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎲 Game Board (Host)
- **Numbers 1–90** laid out on a live 15-column board
- **One-click "Next Number"**, random with no repeats
- **Called numbers light up** with a zoom animation
- **Winner tracker** for First Row, Second Row, Third Row and Full House
- **Auto-saves progress**, so a refresh won't wreck your game
- **One-tap reset** for a fresh round
- Responsive layout that works on desktop, tablet and mobile

### 🎫 Ticket (Player)
- **Instant 3×9 ticket** with 5 numbers per row
- **Column-aware numbers** (1–10, 11–20, … 81–90)
- **Tap to cross** a number, tap again to undo
- **Unique Ticket ID** per player
- **Your ticket is remembered** on your device, so closing the tab is safe
- **Reset Ticket** whenever you want a new one

## 🚀 How to Play

1. **Host:** open 👉 [Game Board](https://hclpandv.github.io/tambola/) and share your screen or cast it to a TV.
2. **Players:** open 👉 [Ticket page](https://hclpandv.github.io/tambola/tkt.html) on their phones.
3. Host presses **Next Number** and announces it.
4. Players tap the number on their ticket if they have it ✅
5. First to complete a row or a full house shouts **"Tambola!"** 🎊
6. Host types the winner's name into the winners panel.

## 🏆 Winning Patterns

| Prize | How to win |
|---|---|
| 🥇 First Row | Cross off all numbers in row 1 |
| 🥈 Second Row | Cross off all numbers in row 2 |
| 🥉 Third Row | Cross off all numbers in row 3 |
| 👑 Full House | Cross off every number on your ticket |
